# Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

---

# Purpose

Provides end-to-end traceability from business requirements to implementation and testing.

---

# Traceability Model

```text
Business Requirement
       ↓
Epic
       ↓
Feature
       ↓
User Story
       ↓
Service
       ↓
Test Case
```

---

# RTM

| Requirement ID | Business Requirement | Epic | Service |
|----------------|----------------------|--------|---------|
| BR-001 | Reduce Claim Processing Time | Claims Management | Claims Service |
| BR-002 | Improve Customer Experience | Customer Management | Customer Service |
| BR-003 | Improve Fraud Detection | Fraud Management | Fraud Service |
| BR-004 | Enable AI Automation | AI Platform | AI Service |
| BR-005 | Measure Business Value | Executive Intelligence | Executive Insights Service |
| BR-006 | Improve Scalability | Cloud Platform | Kubernetes Platform |
| BR-007 | Improve Security | Identity & Security | Identity Service |

---

# Validation

The RTM shall be reviewed during:

- Sprint Planning
- Release Planning
- Architecture Reviews

---

# Success Criteria

Every approved requirement must map to:

- Epic
- Feature
- Service
- Test

with no orphaned requirements.
