# Meeting Cadence

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

---

# Purpose

Defines recurring meetings, objectives, participants, and frequency.

---

# Weekly Activities

## Sprint Planning

Frequency:

Every 2 Weeks

Objectives:

- Review backlog
- Define sprint goals
- Select work items

Participants:

- Product Owner
- Project Manager
- Engineering Team

---

## Architecture Review

Frequency:

Bi-Weekly

Objectives:

- Validate architecture decisions
- Review ADRs
- Assess technical risks

Participants:

- Architect
- Engineering Team

---

# Monthly Activities

## Project Health Review

Objectives:

- Review milestones
- Review risks
- Review budget
- Review schedule

Participants:

- PM
- Product Owner
- Architect

---

## Stakeholder Review

Objectives:

- Demonstrate progress
- Discuss roadmap
- Capture feedback

Participants:

- Stakeholders
- Product Owner
- PM

---

# Quarterly Activities

## Executive Steering Committee

Objectives:

- Review strategic goals
- Review benefits realization
- Review ROI

Participants:

- Executive Sponsor
- Product Owner
- PM
- Architect

---

# Key Outputs

Meetings produce:

- Action Items
- Decisions
- Risks
- Architecture Updates
- Backlog Updates

---

# Success Criteria

Meetings remain:

- Action-Oriented
- Transparent
- Value Focused
- Efficient
