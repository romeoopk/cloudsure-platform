# Purpose

The purpose of this Risk Management Plan is to define the processes, responsibilities, methodologies, and governance required to identify, assess, monitor, respond to, and control project risks throughout the CloudSure project lifecycle.

Effective risk management supports successful project delivery by minimizing threats and maximizing opportunities.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of risk management are to:

- Identify risks early.
- Reduce project uncertainty.
- Protect project delivery timelines.
- Minimize financial impacts.
- Improve decision making.
- Increase project success probability.
- Ensure transparent reporting.

---

# Risk Management Approach

CloudSure adopts a proactive risk management approach.

The project team will continuously:

```text
Identify Risks
      ↓
Analyze Risks
      ↓
Plan Responses
      ↓
Implement Responses
      ↓
Monitor Risks
      ↓
Review & Improve
```

Risk management activities will be performed throughout the project lifecycle.

- Sprint Planning
- Sprint Reviews
- Retrospectives

- Risk Reviews

---

# Probability Scale

| Rating | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Low | Unlikely to occur |
| Medium | May occur |
| High | Likely to occur |

---

# Impact Scale

| Rating | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Low | Minimal impact |
| Medium | Moderate impact |
| High | Significant impact |

---

# Risk Scoring

Risk priority is determined using:

```text
Probability × Impact
```

Examples:

High × High = Critical

Medium × High = High

Low × Medium = Low

---

# Risk Response Strategies

## Avoid

Eliminate the risk.

Example:

Avoid unnecessary technology adoption.

---

## Mitigate

Reduce likelihood or impact.

Example:

Introduce new technologies incrementally.

---

## Transfer

Transfer ownership to another party.

Example:

Use managed cloud services.

---

## Accept

Accept and monitor.

Example:

Low-impact risks.

---

# Risk Ownership

Every identified risk must have:

- Risk Owner
- Mitigation Plan
- Review Date

The Risk Owner is responsible for monitoring and reporting risk status.

---

# Risk Review Process

## Weekly Reviews

Conducted during:

- Sprint Planning
- Sprint Review

Activities:

- Review open risks
- Update risk status
- Add new risks

---

## Monthly Reviews

Conducted by:

- Project Manager
- Product Owner
- Architecture Lead

Activities:

- Reassess priorities
- Validate mitigation activities
- Escalate critical risks

---

## Quarterly Reviews

Conducted with:

- Executive Sponsor
- Steering Committee

Activities:

- Strategic risk assessment
- Program health review

---

# Risk Escalation Criteria

A risk shall be escalated if:

- Probability becomes High and Impact becomes High.
- Risk threatens a milestone delivery.
- Risk impacts budget significantly.
- Security or compliance concerns arise.
- Customer-facing functionality is affected.

---

# Risk Reporting

The following reports shall be maintained:

- Risk Register
- RAID Log
- Weekly Status Reports
- Executive Project Reports

---

# Success Criteria

Risk management is considered effective when:

- Risks are identified early.
- Risk exposure is reduced.
- Escalations occur promptly.
- Mitigation plans are executed.
- Risks are actively monitored.
