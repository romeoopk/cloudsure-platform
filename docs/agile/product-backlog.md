# Product Backlog

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Product Owner  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

The Product Backlog is the single prioritized source of work for the CloudSure platform.

Backlog items are organized by Epic and prioritized according to:

- Business Value
- MVP Requirements
- Technical Dependencies
- Risk Reduction
- Strategic Objectives

The backlog will be continuously refined throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# Prioritization Framework

CloudSure uses the following priority levels:

| Priority | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| P1 | Critical for MVP |
| P2 | High Business Value |
| P3 | Important but not MVP Critical |
| P4 | Future Enhancement |

# Backlog Governance

The Product Backlog shall be reviewed during:

- Backlog Refinement Sessions
- Sprint Planning
- Release Planning
- Product Reviews

The Product Owner is responsible for prioritization and approval of backlog changes.

---

# Success Criteria

The backlog is considered healthy when:

- All work items are prioritized.
- Dependencies are identified.
- MVP scope remains clearly defined.
- Business value is visible for all major features.
- Backlog items are refined before implementation.
