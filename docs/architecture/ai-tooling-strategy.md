# AI Tooling Strategy

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

Define how AI agents interact with CloudSure services using Spring AI tool calling.

The AI layer must never bypass business services.

All business data must be accessed through controlled tools.

---

# Tool Calling Architecture

```text
Agent

   |

Spring AI Tool Calling

   |

Business Tools

   |

Business Services

   |

Databases
```

---

# Tool Design Principles

## Principle 1

Every tool maps to a business capability.

---

## Principle 2

Tools cannot modify business data directly.

---

## Principle 3

Tools must be auditable.

---

## Principle 4

Tools should return structured outputs.

# Tool Calling Workflow

Example:

```text
Does my policy cover water damage?
```

Agent performs:

```text
1. Retrieve Policy

2. Search Coverage Documents

3. Retrieve Relevant Clauses

4. Generate Answer
```

---

# Governance

## Allowed Actions

- Information Retrieval
- Recommendations
- Analysis
- Summarization

---

## Prohibited Actions

- Claim Approval
- Claim Rejection
- Policy Cancellation
- Customer Modification

Those actions remain under business service control.
