# Product Epics

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines the major business initiatives (epics) required to deliver CloudSure.

---

# Epic 1: Identity & Security

## Goal

Provide secure authentication, authorization, and identity management.

### Features

- User Registration
- Login
- JWT Authentication
- Role-Based Access Control
- Password Management
- OAuth2 Integration

---

# Epic 2: Customer Management

## Goal

Manage customer information and preferences.

### Features

- Customer Profiles
- Contact Information
- Address Management
- Preferences Management

---

# Epic 3: Policy Management

## Goal

Manage the insurance policy lifecycle.

### Features

- Quote Generation
- Policy Creation
- Policy Renewal
- Policy Cancellation
- Coverage Management

# Epic 4: Claims Management

## Goal

- Claim Submission
- Claim Tracking
- Status Updates
- Adjuster Assignment
- Claim Settlement

- Document Download
