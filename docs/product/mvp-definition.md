# MVP Definition

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

---

# Purpose

Define the minimum viable product (MVP) required to deliver business value while minimizing complexity and risk.

---

# MVP Goals

The MVP must demonstrate:

- Core insurance capabilities
- Cloud-native architecture
- Event-driven architecture
- AI-assisted processing
- Executive insights

---

# In Scope

## Identity Service

- User Registration
- Login
- JWT Authentication
- Role Management

---

## Customer Service

- Customer Profiles
- Address Management

---

## Policy Service

- Create Policies
- View Policies
- Basic Quote Generation

---

## Claims Service

- Create Claims
- Track Claims
- Update Claim Status

---

## Document Service

- Upload Files
- Store Metadata

---

## Event Platform

- Kafka
- Claim Events
- Policy Events

---

## AI Platform

- Spring AI
- Basic RAG
- Claim Summary Generation
- Insurance Copilot

---

## Executive Dashboard

- Claims Processed
- Cost Savings
- Hours Saved
- AI Utilization

---

## Observability

- Prometheus
- Grafana
- OpenTelemetry

---

## Infrastructure

- Docker
- Kubernetes
- Helm
- GitHub Actions

---

# Out of Scope

- Mobile Apps
- Advanced Fraud Detection
- Underwriting Agent
- Multi-Region Deployment
- Active-Active Architecture
- Multi-Cloud Deployment

---

# MVP Success Criteria

## Technical

- Platform deployed to Kubernetes
- Automated CI/CD operational
- Observability operational

---

## Business

- Claims can be submitted end-to-end
- Executive KPI Dashboard operational
- AI claim summaries generated
