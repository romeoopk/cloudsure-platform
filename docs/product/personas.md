# User Personas

**Project:** CloudSure - AI Native Insurance Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas that will interact with CloudSure.

Personas help ensure the platform is designed around real business needs and user outcomes.

---

# Persona 1: Customer

## Description

Insurance policy holder purchasing and managing insurance products.

---

## Responsibilities

- Purchase insurance policies
- View policy coverage
- Submit claims
- Upload supporting documents
- Track claim progress
- Communicate with customer support

---

## Goals

- Fast and simple digital experience
- Transparency into policy coverage
- Quick claim resolution
- 24/7 access to information

---

## Pain Points

- Slow claim processing
- Complex policy documents
- Long customer support wait times
- Lack of claim visibility

---

## Success Criteria

- Self-service capabilities
- AI-powered assistance
- Real-time claim tracking

---

# Persona 2: Claims Adjuster

## Description

Insurance employee responsible for reviewing and processing claims.

---

## Responsibilities

- Review claims
- Validate documentation
- Investigate claim legitimacy
- Approve or reject claims
- Communicate with customers

---

## Goals

- Reduce manual processing effort
- Increase claim throughput
- Detect potential fraud
- Improve decision quality

---

## Pain Points

- Large claim volumes
- Repetitive document review
- Time-consuming investigations
- Limited fraud indicators

---

## Success Criteria

- AI-assisted claim summaries
- Fraud scoring support
- Centralized claim visibility

---

# Persona 3: Insurance Agent

## Description

Representative responsible for policies and customer onboarding.

---

## Responsibilities

- Create quotes
- Issue policies
- Assist customers
- Review policy coverage

---

## Goals

- Faster quote generation
- Improved customer experience
- Increased policy sales

---

## Pain Points

- Manual quote calculations
- Administrative overhead

---

## Success Criteria

- Automated quote generation
- Unified customer information

---
